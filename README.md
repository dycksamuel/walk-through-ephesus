# Walk Through Ephesus

An explorable 3D recreation of Ephesus as it stood in the apostle Paul's day (Acts 19–20), made for a youth group studying Ephesians.

**Status:** Phase 1 of 5, a walkable layout of the city. See `PLAN.md` for what is coming.

## Play

Open the link in any modern browser, on a phone or a laptop. Nothing to install and no account needed.

- **Phone:** left thumb on the joystick to walk; drag anywhere else to look around; tap the map to open it.
- **Laptop:** W A S D or arrow keys to walk; Shift to jog; click and drag to look around; M for the map.

## Edit the words

All text is in the `content` folder. On github.com, open a file there, click the pencil, change the words between the quotation marks, and commit. The live game updates about a minute later.
